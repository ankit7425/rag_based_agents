INDIAN PREMIER LEAGUE (IPL) - OVERVIEW

Introduction The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20
(T20) cricket league organized by the Board of Control for Cricket in
India (BCCI). It began in 2008 and is one of the most popular and
valuable cricket leagues in the world.

Key Facts - Organizer: BCCI - Format: Twenty20 (T20) - Started: 2008 -
Usually held from March to May - Franchise-based tournament with teams
representing Indian cities

Current Teams - Chennai Super Kings (CSK) - Mumbai Indians (MI) - Royal
Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) - Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) - Delhi
Capitals (DC) - Rajasthan Royals (RR) - Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) -
Punjab Kings (PBKS) - Lucknow Super Giants (LSG) - Gujarat Titans (GT)

Tournament Format 1. League Stage 2. Qualifier 1 3. Eliminator 4.
Qualifier 2 5. Final

Why IPL is Popular - High-quality cricket - International and Indian
star players - Massive fan following - Advanced statistics and
analytics - Entertainment and broadcasting
